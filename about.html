Meet the Main Trio

The story primarily centers around three cheerful yet hard-working friends who explore their unique world, battle soft monsters, and earn rewards through exams and jobs.

Chiikawa

The Gentle Hero

Chiikawa is gentle, sweet, and kind. Cautious to step out of their comfort zone, and somewhat shy, Chiikawa often gets nervous which usually results in anxious tears. Thankfully, best friends Hachiware and Usagi always has this cute and small creature’s back.

Hachiware

The Cheerful Guide

Hachiware has a big heart, possesses a can-do attitude, and always finds the bright side. A true best friend, Hachiware helps Chiikawa overcome fears and makes them feel safe. Despite the cat-like ears and the occasional blep, for the record, Hachiware is not necessarily a cat.

Usagi

The Fearless Chaos

The most energetic of the crew, Usagi seems to find the fun. Though the word usagi literally means rabbit in Japanese, and Usagi has bunny-looking ears, it’s unclear Usagi’s identity. Maybe a rabbit, maybe not. Lively and fearless, Usagi is known for signature yells “Yaha!” and “Ura!”. Interestingly, no one knows where Usagi lives.

Key Milestones

  1. 2020 — First webcomic appearance posted on Nagano's Twitter account
  2. 2021 — Rapid rise in popularity across Japan
  3. 2022 — Official manga publishing and TV anime adaptation broadcast
  4. 2023 — Global merchandise releases, plushies, and pop-up stores
  5. 2024 — Ongoing manga chapters and worldwide fan community growth

Key World Facts

  • Characters earn income by doing weeding and pesticide spraying jobs.
  • They can study to earn official certification licenses for better work.
  • Food often naturally sprouts from the ground, such as rice or ramen.
  • Despite being cute, the world features mysterious and dangerous monsters!

The charm of Chiikawa lies in how these tiny, cute creatures work genuinely hard through anxieties and challenges to enjoy small happy moments with each other.
